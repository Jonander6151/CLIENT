🎵 Song of the Year Awards

Vote for your favorite song of the year

Welcome to the Song of the Year Awards platform, a music celebration where fans from all around the world can vote for their favorite hit songs. This interactive platform allows users to explore some of the most popular tracks of the year and support the artists they love the most.

Each song card includes the album cover, the title of the song, the artist name and a voting button. Every time a user votes for a song, the total number of votes increases and a new star is added to visually represent the score obtained by that song during the contest.

The goal of this project is not only to practice JavaScript programming concepts such as arrays, objects, loops, DOM manipulation and events, but also to improve the accessibility and user experience of a modern web application.
